Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 10 November 2026

At Sacchi Flyier, we respect the privacy of our customers and website visitors. Protecting personal information is important to us, and we aim to handle it responsibly and transparently.

This Privacy Policy explains how we may collect, use, store, share, and protect personal information when customers visit our website, place an order, contact our support team, or otherwise interact with our services.

By using our website, customers acknowledge that their information may be handled as described in this policy, subject to applicable privacy and data protection laws.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Depending on how customers interact with our website and services, we may collect the following types of information:

A. Personal and Contact Information

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Telephone or mobile number.
  • Billing and shipping address.
  • Country, city, postal code, and delivery details.

B. Order and Transaction Information

  • Products purchased and order details.
  • Order number and transaction reference.
  • Payment status and payment confirmation.
  • Shipping, delivery, and tracking information.
  • Return, refund, replacement, and customer support records.

C. Technical and Website Information

When visitors access our website, certain technical information may be collected, depending on the tools and services enabled on our website. This may include:

  • IP address.
  • Browser type and device information.
  • Operating system.
  • Pages visited and website interaction information.
  • Approximate location derived from technical information.
  • Cookies and similar technologies, where enabled.

2. HOW WE COLLECT YOUR INFORMATION

We may collect information through different methods, including:

  • When customers place an order on our website.
  • When customers submit forms or contact our support team.
  • When customers subscribe to newsletters or marketing communications, where offered.
  • When customers communicate with us by email or telephone.
  • Through website technologies such as cookies, where enabled.
  • Through payment, shipping, and fulfilment service providers when they process an order.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We may use collected information for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Processing and managing customer orders.
  • Arranging shipping, delivery, and fulfilment.
  • Communicating order confirmations and updates.
  • Responding to customer questions and support requests.
  • Handling returns, refunds, replacements, and complaints.
  • Processing payments through available payment providers.
  • Improving our website, products, and customer experience.
  • Detecting and preventing fraud, misuse, or unauthorised activity.
  • Meeting applicable legal, regulatory, and accounting requirements.
  • Sending promotional communications where permitted and, where required, with customer consent.

4. PAYMENT INFORMATION

Payments made through our website may be processed by third-party payment providers.

Depending on the payment method, customers may be required to provide payment details directly to the relevant payment provider.

We may receive payment confirmations, transaction references, payment status, and other information necessary to manage orders and customer support.

Payment providers handle payment information according to their own privacy policies, security practices, and applicable requirements.

5. SHARING OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

We do not share personal information indiscriminately. However, we may share necessary information with trusted third parties when required to operate our business and provide our services.

These parties may include:

  • Payment Providers: To process payments, verify transactions, and manage refunds.
  • Suppliers and Fulfilment Partners: To prepare, process, and fulfil customer orders.
  • Shipping and Logistics Companies: To arrange delivery, provide tracking, and resolve shipping issues.
  • Technology and Website Service Providers: To support website hosting, maintenance, security, and related services.
  • Professional Advisers: Where necessary for legal, accounting, or business advice.
  • Government and Legal Authorities: Where disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

We expect relevant service providers to handle personal information responsibly and only for appropriate purposes.

6. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

Sacchi Flyier serves customers in different countries. To process international orders, certain information may need to be transferred to or accessed by service providers, suppliers, or fulfilment partners located outside the customer's country.

These transfers may be necessary for payment processing, order fulfilment, shipping, customer support, or other services related to an order.

Where required, we will take appropriate steps to handle international transfers in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

7. COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies, depending on the features and services enabled.

These technologies may help us to:

  • Support website functionality.
  • Remember certain website preferences.
  • Understand website performance and visitor activity, where analytics tools are enabled.
  • Improve website navigation and user experience.

Customers may be able to manage or disable certain cookies through their browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect website functionality.

Where required by law, we will request consent before using cookies or similar technologies that require it.

8. DATA SECURITY

We take reasonable measures to help protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

These measures may include appropriate technical, administrative, and organisational safeguards.

However, no website, internet transmission, or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted or stored electronically.

9. DATA RETENTION

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including order processing, customer support, business operations, dispute resolution, and legal or regulatory requirements.

When information is no longer required, we may securely delete it, anonymise it, or otherwise dispose of it in accordance with applicable law.

10. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on the laws applicable to the customer's location, customers may have certain rights regarding their personal information.

These rights may include:

  • Requesting access to personal information we hold.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Requesting deletion of personal information where legally available.
  • Withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Objecting to or restricting certain processing activities where applicable.
  • Requesting information about how personal information is used or shared.
  • Unsubscribing from promotional emails.
  • Submitting a privacy-related complaint to the appropriate authority where applicable.

To exercise an applicable privacy right, customers may contact us using the email address provided in the Contact Us section. We may need to verify the customer's identity before responding to certain requests.

11. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our website and services are not intentionally directed towards children who are not legally permitted to use such services under applicable law.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children without any consent or authorisation required by law.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child in circumstances where it should not have been collected, we will take appropriate steps in accordance with applicable law.

12. THIRD-PARTY WEBSITES AND SERVICES

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, payment services, or other external platforms.

These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We do not control their privacy practices or the information they independently collect.

Customers should review the privacy policies of third-party services before providing personal information to them.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business operations, website features, service providers, privacy practices, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised last-updated date.

Customers are encouraged to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we handle personal information.
