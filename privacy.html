# Privacy Policy **Last Updated: 10 November 2026** At Sacchi Flyier, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information of our customers and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, process, and share personal information when you visit our website, place an order, or communicate with our customer support team. As an international online store, we may work with third-party payment providers, suppliers, fulfilment partners, and shipping companies to process and deliver customer orders. We handle personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable privacy and data protection laws. ## 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT We may collect the following types of personal information when customers use our website or place an order: - **Personal details:** Name, email address, and phone number. - **Shipping information:** Delivery address, country, city, state, and postal code. - **Billing information:** Billing name and billing address. - **Order information:** Products purchased, order number, order history, and transaction status. - **Payment information:** Payment confirmation and transaction-related details provided by our payment service providers. - **Technical information:** IP address, browser type, device information, and website usage information, where collected. We only collect information that is reasonably necessary for our business operations and the services we provide. ## 2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION We may use the information we collect to: - Process and confirm customer orders. - Arrange product fulfilment and delivery. - Communicate order updates and shipping information. - Process payments, refunds, returns, and cancellations. - Respond to customer enquiries and provide support. - Prevent fraudulent transactions and protect our website. - Improve our website, products, and customer experience. - Meet applicable legal, regulatory, and tax requirements. - Send promotional emails or offers where permitted by law and, where required, with customer consent. ## 3. PAYMENT INFORMATION Payments made through our website may be processed by third-party payment service providers. We do not intend to store complete debit card numbers, credit card numbers, UPI PINs, passwords, or banking credentials on our own website servers. Payment providers may collect and process payment information according to their own privacy policies and security practices. ## 4. SHARING OF PERSONAL INFORMATION We may share necessary customer information with trusted third parties when required to operate our business and fulfil orders. These parties may include: - Payment processing providers. - Shipping and courier companies. - Product suppliers and fulfilment partners. - Website hosting and technology providers. - Customer support and communication service providers. - Government authorities or legal organisations when required by law. We only share information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose. **We do not sell customers' personal information to third parties.** ## 5. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS Because we serve customers worldwide and may work with international suppliers and fulfilment partners, personal information may be processed or transferred to countries outside the customer's country of residence. Where required, we will take appropriate steps to protect personal information in accordance with applicable data protection laws. ## 6. COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to: - Improve website functionality. - Remember customer preferences. - Understand website traffic and usage. - Improve the shopping experience. - Support security and website performance. Customers can manage cookie preferences through available website controls and browser settings. Where required by law, we will request consent before using non-essential cookies. For more information, please read our Cookie Policy. ## 7. DATA SECURITY We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, misuse, loss, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Customers should also take reasonable steps to protect their own devices, accounts, and personal information. ## 8. DATA RETENTION We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. Some information may need to be retained for order records, accounting, tax requirements, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, or other legal obligations. When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal requirements. ## 9. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS Depending on the laws applicable to your location, you may have the right to: - Request access to your personal information. - Request correction of inaccurate information. - Request deletion of personal information where legally permitted. - Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. - Object to certain types of data processing. - Request information about how your personal information is used. - Unsubscribe from promotional communications. To exercise your applicable rights, please contact us using the details in the Contact Us section below. ## 10.
