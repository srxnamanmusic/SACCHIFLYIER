Cookie Policy

Last Updated: 10 November 2026

At Sacchi Flyier, we aim to provide visitors with a smooth, secure, and convenient website experience.

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar technologies are, how our website may use them, and what choices visitors may have when managing cookies.

The cookies and technologies actually used on our website depend on the features, services, and tools that we enable.

1. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on a visitor's device, such as a computer, tablet, or mobile phone.

They can help websites remember certain information, recognise a browser, maintain website functionality, and improve the browsing experience.

Similar technologies may include pixels, local storage, tags, and other tools that store or access information on a visitor's device.

2. WHY WE MAY USE COOKIES

Depending on the technologies enabled on our website, we may use cookies for purposes such as:

  • Supporting essential website functionality.
  • Remembering certain visitor preferences.
  • Supporting shopping cart and checkout functions.
  • Helping maintain website security.
  • Understanding website performance where analytics tools are enabled.
  • Improving website navigation and user experience.
  • Supporting advertising or marketing features, where enabled and permitted.

3. TYPES OF COOKIES

Cookies may be grouped into different categories depending on their purpose.

A. Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies may be required for basic website functions, such as security, navigation, shopping cart operation, or checkout.

Where permitted by law, strictly necessary cookies may operate without consent because they are needed to provide a service requested by the visitor.

B. Functional Cookies

These cookies may help remember preferences or settings so visitors can enjoy a more convenient browsing experience.

C. Analytics and Performance Cookies

If analytics tools are enabled, these cookies may help us understand how visitors use our website, which pages are visited, and how website performance can be improved.

D. Advertising and Marketing Cookies

If advertising or marketing technologies are enabled, these cookies may be used to measure advertising performance or provide relevant marketing content.

We will obtain consent where required by applicable law.

4. FIRST-PARTY AND THIRD-PARTY COOKIES

First-party cookies are set directly by the website a visitor is accessing.

Third-party cookies may be set by external services integrated into a website, such as analytics, payment, advertising, or other service providers.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and practices.

The specific third-party cookies used on our website may change as our website features and service providers change.

5. HOW LONG COOKIES REMAIN ON YOUR DEVICE

Cookies may remain on a visitor's device for different periods depending on their purpose.

Session Cookies

These cookies are generally removed when the visitor closes their browser.

Persistent Cookies

These cookies may remain on the visitor's device until they expire or are deleted manually.

The duration of individual cookies depends on the relevant cookie and the service that sets it.

6. MANAGING YOUR COOKIE PREFERENCES

Visitors may be able to manage cookies through their browser settings.

Depending on the browser, available controls may allow visitors to:

  • View stored cookies.
  • Delete existing cookies.
  • Block certain or all cookies.
  • Receive notifications when cookies are being stored.
  • Set preferences for individual websites.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality, including shopping cart features, checkout, or saved preferences.

7. CONSENT AND COOKIE CHOICES

Where required by applicable law, we will request consent before placing or accessing cookies that are not strictly necessary.

Visitors may be able to accept, reject, or customise optional cookies through a cookie preference tool if one is provided on our website.

Visitors may also withdraw or change their consent where the applicable law provides that right.

8. PERSONAL INFORMATION AND COOKIES

Some cookies or similar technologies may collect information that can be associated with an individual or device.

Where cookie-related information constitutes personal information, we handle it in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable data protection laws.

9. THIRD-PARTY WEBSITES

Our website may contain links to external websites or services.

Those websites may use their own cookies and tracking technologies. We do not control the cookie practices of external websites.

Visitors should review the relevant third-party cookie and privacy policies before using those services.

10. CHANGES TO THIS COOKIE POLICY

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, technologies, service providers, business practices, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised last-updated date.
