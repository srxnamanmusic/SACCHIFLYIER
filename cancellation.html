Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: 11 November 2026

At Sacchi Flyier, we understand that customers may sometimes need to cancel an order. This Cancellation Policy explains how cancellation requests are handled and what customers can expect when requesting an order cancellation.

1. Requesting an Order Cancellation

Customers who wish to cancel an order should contact our customer support team as soon as possible after placing the order.

Please include the following information:

  • Order number
  • Name used when placing the order
  • Email address associated with the order
  • Reason for requesting cancellation

2. Cancellation Before Order Processing

Cancellation requests received before an order has entered processing or fulfilment may be eligible for cancellation.

We will review the request and confirm whether the order can be cancelled.

3. Orders Already Processing

Once an order has entered processing, been submitted for fulfilment, or been prepared for dispatch, we may not be able to stop or cancel it.

Cancellation eligibility depends on the order's fulfilment status and whether the relevant fulfilment arrangements can still be stopped.

4. Orders Already Shipped

Orders that have already been shipped or handed over to a delivery provider generally cannot be cancelled through the normal cancellation process.

Customers may contact us for assistance. Any return, refund or other available remedy will be handled according to our Return & Refund Policy and applicable consumer protection laws.

5. Cancellation by Sacchi Flyier

We reserve the right to cancel an order where reasonably necessary, including circumstances such as:

  • Product unavailability or stock issues
  • Incorrect or incomplete order information
  • Pricing or listing errors
  • Payment verification or transaction problems
  • Suspected fraudulent or unauthorised transactions
  • Shipping or fulfilment restrictions
  • Other legitimate operational or legal reasons

Where we cancel a paid order, we will communicate the cancellation and arrange any applicable refund in accordance with our policies and applicable law.

6. Refunds for Cancelled Orders

If a cancellation is approved and payment has already been collected, any applicable refund will be processed through the original payment method where reasonably possible.

Refund processing and the time taken for the funds to appear in a customer's account may depend on the payment provider and financial institution.

For further information, please read our Return & Refund Policy.

7. Cash on Delivery Orders

Customers who placed an order using Cash on Delivery should contact our support team immediately if they wish to request cancellation.

Cancellation will depend on the current order and fulfilment status. Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible.

8. Changes to an Existing Order

Requests to change a product, quantity, delivery address or other order details should be submitted as soon as possible.

We cannot guarantee that changes will be possible after an order has entered processing or fulfilment.

9. Duplicate or Incorrect Orders

If a customer accidentally places a duplicate order or notices incorrect information, they should contact us immediately with the relevant order numbers and details.

We will review the request and assist where cancellation or correction is still possible.

10. Important Customer Information

Submitting a cancellation request does not automatically mean that an order has been cancelled. Customers should wait for confirmation from our team.

Nothing in this policy limits any cancellation, withdrawal, refund or other rights that customers may have under applicable consumer protection laws.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cancellation Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business, fulfilment arrangements or legal requirements.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised Last Updated date.

12. Contact Us

To request an order cancellation or ask questions about this policy, please contact our team.
