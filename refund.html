# Return & Refund Policy **Last Updated: 10 November 2026** At Sacchi Flyier, customer satisfaction is important to us. We are committed to providing a transparent, reliable, and smooth shopping experience to customers worldwide. This Return & Refund Policy explains the conditions and procedures for requesting returns, refunds, replacements, or other resolutions for orders placed through our website. All requests are reviewed individually based on the condition of the product, the nature of the issue, available supporting evidence, shipping and fulfilment conditions, and applicable consumer protection laws. Nothing in this policy is intended to limit or exclude any rights that customers may have under applicable law. ## 1. ELIGIBLE RETURN, REFUND OR REPLACEMENT CASES Customers may contact us if: - The product received is damaged. - The wrong product was received. - A product or required part is missing. - The order is confirmed as lost. - The product has a verified manufacturing defect. - The product received is significantly different from its description. - Another issue occurs that qualifies for a remedy under applicable law. After reviewing the request and supporting evidence, we may offer an appropriate resolution, such as a refund, replacement, resend, or another remedy. ## 2. REPORTING A PROBLEM AND TIME LIMIT Customers should contact us as soon as reasonably possible after discovering a problem with an order. For damaged, incorrect, or missing products, we recommend contacting us within 7 days of delivery so that we can investigate the issue promptly. This recommended reporting period does not remove or reduce any mandatory consumer rights or legal time limits. ## 3. PROOF AND SUPPORTING EVIDENCE To review a return, refund, or replacement request, we may ask customers to provide: - Order number. - Customer name and registered email address. - Clear photographs or videos of the product. - Photographs of the packaging and shipping label, where relevant. - A description of the issue. - Other reasonable supporting information needed to investigate the claim. We use this information to assess the request and coordinate with our fulfilment or shipping partners when necessary. ## 4. RETURN REQUEST PROCEDURE To request a return, refund, or replacement, customers should contact our support team at: **Email:** info@sacchiflyier.in Please include the order number, a description of the problem, and supporting photographs or videos where applicable. Our team will review the request and provide further instructions. Customers should not send products to any address without receiving return instructions from us. ## 5. RETURN SHIPPING If a return is approved and required, we will provide the applicable return instructions and designated return address. Responsibility for return shipping costs will depend on the reason for the return, the applicable circumstances, and consumer protection laws. If a product is damaged, defective, incorrect, or otherwise qualifies for a remedy, we will handle the matter in accordance with applicable law. ## 6. CHANGE OF MIND, WRONG SIZE OR INCORRECT ORDER If a customer changes their mind, orders the wrong size or colour, or no longer wants a product, return or refund eligibility will depend on the product, its condition, our applicable return arrangements, and the laws of the customer's country. Customers should contact us before returning an item. Nothing in this section excludes any cancellation or return rights that customers may have under applicable law. ## 7. REFUND PROCESSING Once a refund is approved, we will generally process it through the original payment method, where possible. The time required for the refund to appear in the customer's account may depend on the payment provider, bank, or financial institution. We will inform customers if additional information is required to complete an approved refund. ## 8. LOST, DELAYED OR UNDELIVERED ORDERS If an order appears to be lost, significantly delayed, or undelivered, customers should contact us. We may investigate the shipment with our shipping or fulfilment partners and review the available tracking information. Depending on the investigation and applicable law, we may offer a replacement, resend, refund, or another appropriate resolution. Estimated delivery times are not guaranteed delivery dates unless expressly stated otherwise. ## 9. CUSTOMS DUTIES AND IMPORT TAXES International orders may be subject to customs duties, import taxes, handling fees, or other charges imposed by the destination country. Unless otherwise stated at checkout, these charges may be the customer's responsibility. If a shipment is delayed or returned because required customs charges or import procedures have not been completed, we will review the circumstances individually and handle the matter in accordance with applicable law. ## 10. NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS Certain products may not be eligible for return because of their nature, condition, hygiene requirements, or applicable legal restrictions. Where applicable, any non-returnable product conditions will be communicated on the relevant product page or before purchase. This section does not restrict mandatory rights available to customers under applicable law. ## 11. ORDER CANCELLATIONS Customers who wish to cancel an order should contact us as soon as possible. Cancellation requests will depend on the order's processing, fulfilment, and shipping status, as well as applicable consumer protection laws.
