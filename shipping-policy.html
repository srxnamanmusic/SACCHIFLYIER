Delivery & Shipping Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Order Processing

Orders are processed after successful payment confirmation. Processing times depend on product availability and supplier requirements.

2. Shipping and Delivery

Sacchi Flyier works with third-party suppliers, including CJdropshipping. Products may be shipped directly from suppliers to customers. International delivery is subject to product availability and destination restrictions.

3. Delivery Estimates

Delivery times vary by destination, shipping method, supplier processing, and customs clearance. Any estimate shown at checkout is not a guaranteed delivery date.

4. Shipping Charges

Applicable shipping charges will be shown before checkout. Charges may vary by product, destination, and shipping method.

5. Order Tracking

Where tracking is available, tracking details will be shared after dispatch. Tracking updates may take time to appear.

6. Customs Duties and Taxes

International orders may incur import duties or taxes. Customers should check local requirements. Responsibility for charges depends on the shipping terms and applicable law.

7. Incorrect Address or Failed Delivery

Customers should provide a complete and accurate delivery address. Contact us if delivery fails. Additional shipping charges may apply where permitted.

8. Delayed, Missing, or Damaged Orders

Email us with your order number if an order is delayed, missing, or damaged. We will review the issue under our Refund & Cancellation Policy and applicable law.
