Shipping Policy

Last Updated: 10 November 2026

At Sacchi Flyier, we aim to provide a smooth and reliable delivery experience to customers worldwide.

This Shipping Policy explains how we process orders, arrange shipping, provide tracking information, and handle delivery enquiries for purchases made through our website.

Orders may be fulfilled through our suppliers and third-party fulfilment or logistics partners. Shipping methods, processing arrangements, and delivery estimates may vary depending on the product and destination country.

1. ORDER PROCESSING

After an order is successfully placed, we begin the order verification and processing procedure.

Processing may include:

  • Reviewing order and payment information.
  • Confirming product availability with our fulfilment partners.
  • Preparing the product for dispatch.
  • Arranging shipping through an available logistics service.

Order processing time is separate from shipping and delivery time. Processing may take longer during holidays, high-demand periods, supplier delays, or other circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

2. SHIPPING DESTINATIONS

Sacchi Flyier aims to serve customers internationally. However, shipping availability may vary depending on the destination, product, logistics provider, and applicable restrictions.

Some countries, regions, islands, or remote locations may have limited shipping options or may not be serviceable.

Customers should check the available shipping options displayed at checkout before completing an order.

We reserve the right to review or cancel an order when delivery cannot reasonably be arranged because of shipping restrictions, unavailable services, or legal requirements. Any applicable refund will be handled in accordance with our Return & Refund Policy and applicable law.

3. SHIPPING METHODS

We may use different shipping and logistics providers depending on product availability, destination, delivery requirements, and available shipping services.

The shipping method may be selected by our fulfilment partners based on the available options for the order.

Shipping services may include standard international shipping or other available delivery services.

The available shipping method and any applicable shipping charges will be communicated during checkout or before the order is confirmed.

4. SHIPPING COSTS

Shipping charges may vary depending on the product, destination country, parcel weight, shipping method, and fulfilment arrangements.

Any applicable shipping cost will be displayed at checkout before the customer completes the purchase.

If shipping is displayed as free, the customer will not be charged a separate shipping fee for that order at checkout, unless otherwise clearly stated.

Customers should review the final order total before completing payment.

5. ESTIMATED DELIVERY TIME

Delivery times may vary depending on the destination, shipping method, supplier processing time, customs procedures, and local delivery services.

Any delivery estimate displayed on our website or communicated during the ordering process is an estimate and not a guaranteed delivery date, unless expressly stated otherwise.

Delays may occur because of:

  • Customs inspections and clearance procedures.
  • Weather conditions or natural events.
  • Public holidays and peak shopping seasons.
  • Airline, transport, or logistics disruptions.
  • Incorrect or incomplete delivery information.
  • Local delivery restrictions or operational delays.
  • Other circumstances outside our reasonable control.

6. ORDER TRACKING

Where tracking is available for an order, tracking information may be provided after the shipment has been dispatched and the tracking details have been made available by the logistics provider.

Customers can use the provided tracking information to check available shipment updates.

Tracking information may take some time to become active after dispatch. Updates may also be delayed or limited depending on the shipping provider and destination.

If tracking information is unavailable or does not appear to update for an extended period, customers should contact our support team.

7. CUSTOMS DUTIES AND IMPORT TAXES

International shipments may be subject to customs duties, import taxes, VAT, handling fees, or other charges imposed by the destination country.

Unless otherwise stated at checkout, these charges may be the customer's responsibility where permitted by applicable law.

Customs charges and import procedures are determined by the relevant authorities in the destination country and are outside our direct control.

Customers are responsible for checking applicable import requirements before placing an international order.

8. SHIPPING ADDRESS AND DELIVERY DETAILS

Customers are responsible for providing accurate and complete shipping information when placing an order.

This may include:

  • Full recipient name.
  • Complete street address.
  • City, state, and postal code.
  • Country.
  • Valid contact telephone number, where required.
  • Any additional delivery information requested at checkout.

Incorrect or incomplete information may result in delivery delays, failed delivery attempts, or returned shipments.

Customers who need to correct their shipping details should contact us as soon as possible. Address changes cannot be guaranteed after an order has entered processing or has been dispatched.

9. FAILED OR MISSED DELIVERIES

A delivery may fail because the recipient is unavailable, the address is incorrect, access to the delivery location is restricted, or the local carrier cannot complete delivery.

Where available, customers should follow the instructions provided by the local delivery provider to arrange another delivery attempt or collect the parcel.

If a shipment is returned or delivery cannot be completed, customers should contact us so that we can review the available information and determine an appropriate resolution in accordance with applicable law.

10. LOST, DAMAGED OR DELAYED SHIPMENTS

If an order appears to be lost, significantly delayed, delivered in damaged condition, or not received, customers should contact us as soon as reasonably possible.

We may request:

  • Order number.
  • Customer name and registered email address.
  • Tracking number, where available.
  • Photographs of damaged packaging or products, where relevant.
  • A description of the delivery issue.

We may investigate the issue with our fulfilment or shipping partners and review the available tracking and delivery information.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, an appropriate resolution may include a replacement, resend, refund, or another remedy.

11. ORDER CANCELLATIONS

Customers who wish to cancel an order should contact us as soon as possible.

Cancellation requests depend on the order's processing, fulfilment, and shipping status.

Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible. However, customers may still have return or refund rights under our Return & Refund Policy and applicable consumer protection laws.

12. SHIPPING RESTRICTIONS

Certain products or destinations may be subject to shipping restrictions, customs requirements, import regulations, or carrier limitations.

We may be unable to ship an order to a particular destination if the product is restricted, the shipping service is unavailable, or delivery would violate applicable laws or regulations.

If an order cannot be fulfilled for these reasons, we will review the circumstances and handle any applicable refund in accordance with our Return & Refund Policy and applicable law.

13. POLICY UPDATES

We may update this Shipping Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our shipping services, fulfilment arrangements, logistics partners, business practices, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised last-updated date.
